Observational Astrophysics
Hands-on sessions: scientific observing projects 
Goal:
develop a scientific project to be elaborated in small groups in which 
students go through the different scientific steps:
 writing an observing proposal 
 planning and performing observations with a telescope
 reducing and analyzing the data
 writing a research report
