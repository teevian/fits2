 ABSTRACT  (max.  100  words)  
Cygnus  X-1  is  a  high-mass  X-ray  binary  system  containing  the  blue  supergiant  star  HDE  226868  
and
 
a
 
stellar-mass
 
black
 
hole.
 
Although
 
the
 
black
 
hole
 
is
 
invisible
 
in
 
optical
 
light,
 
its
 
strong
 
gravitational
 
field
 
distorts
 
the
 
supergiant,
 
producing
 
clear
 
brightness
 
variations
 
throughout
 
its
 
orbit.
 
We
 
propose
 
a
 
4.5-hour
 
CCD
 
observation
 
in
 
the
 
B,
 
V,
 
and
 
R
 
bands
 
to
 
record
 
a
 
segment
 
of
 
the
 
light
 
curve
 
and
 
measure
 
its
 
colour
 
indices.
 
Using
 
differential
 
photometry
 
with
 
comparison
 
stars
 
in
 
the
 
same
 
field
 
will
 
minimise
 
atmospheric
 
and
 
instrumental
 
errors.
 
The
 
resulting
 
measurements
 
will
 
then
 
be
 
compared
 
against
 
the
 
expected
 
ellipsoidal
 
modulation
 
of
 
this
 
system.
