PAGE 16
Telescope mounts
1. Alt-azimuth mount: one axis for azimuth and 
another for altitude. 
2. Simple design but complex two-axis movement 
to follow Earth rotation. 
3. For large telescopes support goes through 
center of mass and there are less torques
4. Equatorial mount (RA,DEC): one axis aligned 
to the rotational axis of Earth (celestial pole). 
5. It can easily follow objects as they move 
through the sky
6. C14 at the CIGeoE has an Equatorial mount
