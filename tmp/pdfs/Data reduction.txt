Bias
● A bias frame is an image with zero exposure time;
● It’s used to obtain the signal produced by the electronics themselves, the 
readout noise.


PAGE 3
Dark
● A dark frame is similar to a bias (no light), but with exposure time (ideally the 
same exposure as the light frame)
● It’s used to read the signal from thermal fluctuations, the dark current


PAGE 4
Flat
● A flat field is an image of a constant illumination through the CCD. 
● It’s used to measure each pixel’s response to light:
● Flats must be taken in all filters.


PAGE 5
Summary
● Bias: Light Exposure Time Filter
● Dark: Light Exposure Time Filter
● Flat: Light Exposure Time Filter

PAGE 6
Stacking
● To increase SNR, we increase exposure time; However, things can saturate.
● Solution: We stack many images of shorter exposures: blend several images 
of the same target together.
● How: create a data cube with all the frames and median (average) in the 3rd 
dimension.
○ Final image is not brighter (signal is the same)!
○ Final image is smoother because it has less noise (noise is random and cancels out)
