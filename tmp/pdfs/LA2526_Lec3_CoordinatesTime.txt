Project Ideas
Nebulae
Scientific questions:
● What is a nebula? What is its chemical composition? How long ago has it exploded?
Skills:
● Measure luminosity profile and integrated luminosity of a nebula
● Obtain a color-composite image
Tips:
● For the exposure time calculation, nebulae are extended sources
● Make sure (most of) the nebula fits in the field of view. Mosaics are possible but challenging.

PAGE 16
Project Ideas
Stellar Clusters
Scientific questions:   
● What is a stellar cluster? What is its age? What is the turnoff point?
Skills: 
● Obtain magnitudes and colors of each star.
● Plot the stars in a Hertzsprung-Russell diagram
● Obtain a color-composite image
Tips:
● To calculate exposure times, use faint stars in stellar cluster. See the member of a cluster with 
SIMBAD: http://simbad.u-strasbg.fr/simbad/ 
● Do not pick stellar clusters that are too crowded so that you can resolve more stars (and do 
photometry)
● Make sure (most of) your stellar cluster fits in the field of view

PAGE 17
Project Ideas
Individual Stars
Scientific questions:
● What are the types of some of the stars we see in the night sky? How will they evolve?
Skills:
● Calculate the color and temperature of stars.
● Plot the stars in a Hertzsprung-Russell diagram
Tips:
● Be careful when selecting stars to ensure that they do not saturate the CCD.

HOW IS THE PROPOSAL PROCESS?
• Observatories/agencies send a call for proposals (twice a 
yr)
• Panel committee evaluates proposals for:  a) scientific case 
and b) technical feasibility and they are ranked (Not all 
members are specialists!)
• Rejection/acceptance is sent and observing priority (A,B,C)
